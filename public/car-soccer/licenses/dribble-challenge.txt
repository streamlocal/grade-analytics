Dribbling Challenge 1 Remastered by Lethamyr
https://lethamyr.com/maps/139

Experimental browser adaptation for local evaluation, requested by the project
owner on September 13, 2026. Original map rights remain with their owners;
Car Soccer does not grant an open-source licence to these assets.

Original geometry, placements, stage spawns and decoded trigger shapes are
adapted to browser rendering and physics. External game materials are replaced
with reconstructed finishes and original generated textures. Authored movement
tracks and attached reset volumes are translated into browser animation data;
Unreal scripts and effects are not executed.
