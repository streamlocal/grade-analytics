The rules behind the boards. What you buy, how placement changes, and the choices you keep.

01The agreement at a glance

You purchase a place in a public, paid-sponsor ranking. Arena display depends on that changing rank. Your first purchase starts at US$5 of ranking credit; later increases start at US$1 of credit. The purchase price is calculated in BRL, with local-currency payment where supported, as explained below. All qualifying payments remain paid when another advertiser overtakes you. In particular, rank 19 and below receives a directory listing but no field placement. Read the visibility and refund limits below before paying.

The checkout summary, these terms and the accepted content policy form the offer. Mandatory legal protections remain available even where a summary does not repeat them. Save or print this version for your records.

03Price, checkout & confirmation

The leaderboard uses US dollars as a common ranking reference. The checkout base price is in Brazilian reais (BRL). Your first purchase adds at least US$5 of ranking credit. Choose a whole-dollar total; later purchases add the difference from your existing net credit, with a minimum US$1 increase. The maximum cumulative target is US$100,000. For example, moving from US$5 to US$12 adds US$7 of credit. A partial refund can leave fractional credit, in which case only the remaining difference is priced.

We calculate each new purchase's BRL base price from that USD credit using the latest available closing USD/BRL selling reference published by Brazil's Central Bank (BCB PTAX). We multiply the USD amount by that reference and round once to the nearest BRL cent, with half cents rounded up. For illustration only, US$5 at a reference of R$5.0918 per dollar gives a base price of R$25.46. This is a pricing reference, not a promise to exchange or redeem a dollar balance. Ranking credit cannot be transferred or cashed out except through an applicable payment refund.

The reference's date, rate and resulting BRL price are saved with your order. We do not open a new payment using a reference older than seven days. Weekends and holidays can use the latest available business-day reference. A created checkout keeps its BRL base price for its validity period, up to 24 hours; a new checkout may have a different price. Stripe's local-currency quote has its own displayed validity. Price changes do not revalue existing ranking credit.

Where eligible, Stripe automatically offers payment in your local currency and also lets you choose BRL. Availability depends on location, currency and payment method; it is not a promise that every currency or card will be supported. Stripe converts our BRL price using its displayed exchange rate, which includes any Adaptive Pricing conversion charge. Review the selected currency, final amount, applicable tax and conversion information before confirming payment. If you choose BRL, your bank may separately convert it or charge its own fees.

There are two price calculations: the USD ranking reference becomes our BRL price, then Stripe may convert BRL to your chosen payment currency. Different reference times, rounding and Stripe's conversion charge mean the final charge may differ from the nominal USD credit or a bank's exchange-rate estimate. Taxes and conversion charges never increase your ranking. Payment is one-time; choosing a higher total does not authorize later automatic charges.

The payment summary and these terms state the price basis; Stripe collects payment details on its hosted checkout. You can leave without paying and cancel an unpaid checkout in sponsorship management. The final amount shown before confirmation is the amount we charge for that purchase. Any tax we add is shown before you pay and does not increase your ranking credit.

MAGMARCAS handles its applicable tax and invoicing obligations with its accountant, separately from Stripe's automatic-tax service. Stripe automatic tax is disabled; that does not mean the service is tax-exempt. A Stripe receipt is not a substitute for a legally required Brazilian tax invoice. Contact the support address above for invoicing and provide the billing details needed for that purpose; we may request missing information. 04How the ranking works

We rank active, eligible sponsorships by the sum of confirmed advertising payments, less refunds. The higher balance comes first. Equal balances are ordered by when our service first confirmed payment for each sponsorship; the earlier confirmation wins. If those timestamps also match, the stored sponsorship identifier breaks the tie. This deterministic calculation does not involve chance.

A sponsor’s earlier payments keep counting, with no daily bidding round or automatic reset. Its first-confirmation time remains its tie-breaker when it tops up. Payment reversals reduce its balance; a bank dispute can suspend the listing while resolved. Removed or zero-balance sponsorships do not appear in the ranking. The balance is an accounting measure of advertising purchased, not a transferable wallet or money available for withdrawal except under the refund terms.

The leaderboard publishes the advertiser name, destination, normalized artwork and net advertising balance. Do not pay if you do not want that information public. We do not sell secret boosts to rank. Buying separate entries or misrepresenting ownership to manipulate placement is prohibited.

All artwork has a 4:1 aspect ratio. Rank 1 has the largest boards, rank 2 slightly smaller boards, and the ribbon panels are smaller again. The top-down illustration shows ribbon panels as thin lines for readability; their actual artwork remains 4:1.

While fewer than 18 sponsors qualify, we assign each its primary positions first. Remaining signs are divided among eligible arena sponsors in proportion to their balances. We take the whole-number part of each share, then award remaining signs to the largest fractional remainders; equal remainders favor higher rank. Extra appearances are interleaved around the arena. With only one sponsor, it occupies all 20 signs. With US$10 at #1 and US$5 at #2, they receive 13 and 7 signs respectively. Once all primary positions are occupied, the extra appearances end.

Extra appearances never replace another sponsor’s primary allocation. Higher contributions, new sponsors, refunds and removals can change both rank and repeat appearances. A new payment does not buy exclusivity, competitor exclusion or a particular period in a given position.

That does not remove rights concerning defective service, misrepresentation, unauthorized payments or other mandatory grounds. Contact support if a button is unavailable or you believe other rights apply.

If we remove a sponsorship for violating the content rules, we hide it and refund all its remaining payments, including tax we collected, without deducting processing fees. A dispute or payment reversal must be coordinated with Stripe or the bank to avoid duplicate reimbursement; it is not a waiver of a valid refund entitlement.

Refunds normally return through the original payment method. We initiate the request promptly; the provider and issuing bank control when it appears, commonly around 5–10 business days for cards. Pending or failed refunds remain our responsibility to resolve. If the original method cannot receive a refund, support will arrange a lawful alternative after verifying ownership. For payments converted by Stripe Adaptive Pricing, Stripe refunds in the currency originally paid, using the original conversion rate; a full refund returns the original payment amount, including its conversion charge. We do not recalculate refunds using today’s reference rate. For partial refunds, the removed USD ranking credit follows the refunded proportion of the original payment. Separate bank charges or bank conversions are controlled by the issuer, without limiting mandatory reimbursement rights.
