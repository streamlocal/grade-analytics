How the ranking works
We rank active, eligible sponsorships by the sum of confirmed advertising payments, less refunds. The higher balance comes first. Equal balances are ordered by when our service first confirmed payment for each sponsorship; the earlier confirmation wins. If those timestamps also match, the stored sponsorship identifier breaks the tie. This deterministic calculation does not involve chance.
A sponsor’s earlier payments keep counting, with no daily bidding round or automatic reset. Its first-confirmation time remains its tie-breaker when it tops up. Payment reversals reduce its balance; a bank dispute can suspend the listing while resolved. Removed or zero-balance sponsorships do not appear in the ranking. The balance is an accounting measure of advertising purchased, not a transferable wallet or money available for withdrawal except under the refund terms.
The leaderboard publishes the advertiser name, destination, normalized artwork and net advertising balance. Do not pay if you do not want that information public. We do not sell secret boosts to rank. Buying separate entries or misrepresenting ownership to manipulate placement is prohibited.
